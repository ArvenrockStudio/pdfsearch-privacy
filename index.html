PDF Search – Privacy Policy

Publisher: Arvenrock Studio
Legal operator: Arzu Uygun Evcan
Last updated: 2 November 2026

PDF Search is designed as an offline-first document OCR and search application. Protecting the privacy of documents processed by the application is a core product principle.

1. Who operates PDF Search?

PDF Search is published under the Arvenrock Studio brand and is operated by Arzu Uygun Evcan, an individual developer.

Privacy and support contact: arvenrockstudio@gmail.com

2. Documents and OCR processing

PDF Search processes supported documents, including PDF and TIFF files, primarily on the user's device. OCR, document search, navigation and supported export operations are designed to work locally.

PDF Search does not operate a backend service for uploading or storing users' documents. Document contents and OCR text are not intentionally transmitted to an Arvenrock Studio server.

3. Information processed by the app

Depending on the features used, the application may temporarily process information such as:

  • PDF or TIFF document contents;
  • OCR text generated from documents;
  • search queries entered by the user;
  • document metadata needed for local processing;
  • temporary local files created for OCR or export operations.

This information is used to provide the requested functionality on the device.

4. Accounts

PDF Search does not currently require users to create an account or sign in.

5. Advertising

The current version of PDF Search does not contain advertising and does not include an advertising SDK.

6. Analytics and tracking

PDF Search does not currently include a dedicated third-party analytics or advertising tracking SDK.

The application uses Google ML Kit for on-device text recognition. Google documents that ML Kit may collect limited diagnostic, device, application, performance or usage-related information depending on the SDK and configuration.

More information is available in Google's official ML Kit data disclosure documentation: ML Kit Android data disclosure .

7. Local storage and temporary files

PDF Search may create temporary or private local copies of documents while performing OCR, search or export operations. These files are stored in application-controlled storage where applicable and are managed by the application's cleanup mechanisms.

Files explicitly exported by the user, such as searchable PDFs or DOCX files, remain in the destination selected by the user and are under the user's control.

8. Original documents

PDF Search is designed to access original source documents in read-only mode. Export operations create new output files rather than modifying the original source document.

9. Data sharing

PDF Search does not intentionally sell users' document contents or OCR text.

The application does not provide document contents to Arvenrock Studio through an application backend, because no such document-storage backend is currently operated for PDF Search.

If a user voluntarily sends a document or other personal information by email when requesting support, that information is processed as part of the support request and is separate from the normal local document-processing behavior of the application.

10. Data retention and deletion

Temporary application-controlled files are retained only as needed for application functionality and are subject to application cleanup behavior.

Users can remove exported files using normal device file-management tools. Uninstalling the application removes application-private data according to Android platform behavior, except for files that the user deliberately exported to shared or user-selected storage.

11. Internet access

The core PDF Search document-processing features are designed to function without an internet connection. The current release does not request general internet or broad storage permissions for document processing.

Opening external resources such as this Privacy Policy is a separate user-initiated action handled through the device's browser or compatible application.

12. Children's privacy

PDF Search is a general document productivity utility and is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

13. Security

PDF Search is designed to minimize access to user data, use private application storage for temporary processing where appropriate, avoid unnecessary permissions, and preserve original documents.

No software can provide an absolute guarantee of security. Security and privacy protections may be updated as the application evolves.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PDF Search functionality, third-party components, legal requirements or data-handling practices change.

The latest version will be published on this page together with its revision date.

PDF Search – Gizlilik Politikası

Yayıncı marka: Arvenrock Studio
İşletmeci: Arzu Uygun Evcan
Son güncelleme: 2 Kasım 2026

PDF Search, çevrimdışı kullanımı ve kullanıcı belgelerinin gizliliğini önceliklendiren bir OCR ve belge arama uygulaması olarak tasarlanmıştır.

Belge işleme

Desteklenen PDF ve TIFF belgeleri, OCR, arama, gezinme ve dışa aktarma işlemleri için esas olarak kullanıcının cihazında işlenir.

PDF Search, kullanıcı belgelerinin yüklenmesi veya saklanması amacıyla Arvenrock Studio tarafından işletilen bir sunucu veya belge depolama hizmeti kullanmamaktadır.

Hesap ve reklam

Uygulamanın mevcut sürümünde kullanıcı hesabı oluşturmak zorunlu değildir ve reklam bulunmamaktadır.

Google ML Kit

PDF Search, cihaz üzerinde metin tanıma amacıyla Google ML Kit kullanmaktadır. Google, kullanılan SDK ve yapılandırmaya bağlı olarak sınırlı tanılama, cihaz, uygulama, performans veya kullanım bilgilerinin işlenebileceğini açıklamaktadır.

Ayrıntılı bilgi: Google ML Kit Android veri açıklaması .

Geçici dosyalar ve dışa aktarma

OCR ve dışa aktarma işlemleri sırasında uygulamaya ait özel depolama alanlarında geçici dosyalar oluşturulabilir. Kullanıcının özellikle dışa aktardığı PDF veya DOCX dosyaları ise kullanıcının seçtiği konumda kalır.

Orijinal belgeler

PDF Search, kaynak belgeleri salt okunur biçimde kullanacak şekilde tasarlanmıştır. Dışa aktarma işlemleri orijinal belgeyi değiştirmek yerine yeni dosyalar oluşturur.

Destek iletişimi

Kullanıcının destek amacıyla kendi isteğiyle e-posta üzerinden belge veya kişisel bilgi göndermesi, uygulamanın normal cihaz-içi belge işleme sürecinden ayrı bir kullanıcı işlemi olarak değerlendirilir.
